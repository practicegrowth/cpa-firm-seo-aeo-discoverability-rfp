## 3. Scope of Work

_Vendors should propose solutions across the following categories. Indicate which are priorities for your firm._

### 3.1 Technical SEO

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Site speed optimization (Core Web Vitals: LCP, FID/INP, CLS)
- Mobile-first responsiveness audit and fixes
- Structured data / schema markup implementation (Organization, LocalBusiness, ProfessionalService, FAQ, HowTo, Article, BreadcrumbList)
- Crawlability and indexation audit (robots.txt, XML sitemap, canonical tags)
- HTTPS/security configuration
- JavaScript rendering and crawl budget optimization
- Internal linking architecture
- Duplicate content resolution
- Log file analysis (if applicable)
- Migration support (if site redesign is planned)

### 3.2 On-Page SEO

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Title tag optimization (service pages, location pages, blog)
- Meta description optimization
- Header hierarchy (H1-H6) optimization
- Content optimization for target keywords
- Internal linking strategy
- Image optimization (alt text, compression, lazy loading)
- URL structure optimization
- Content freshness updates

### 3.3 Local Search Optimization

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Google Business Profile optimization (categories, services, posts, Q&A, photos)
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency audit across directories
- Citation building and cleanup (industry-specific and general directories)
- Local landing page creation (service-area pages, location-specific content)
- Local pack / Map Pack ranking tracking
- Competitor local SEO analysis
- Review generation strategy (ethical solicitation, response templates)
- Local schema markup (LocalBusiness, GeoCoordinates)

### 3.4 Content Strategy

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Topic cluster development (pillar pages + supporting content)
- Blog/editorial calendar (topic ideation, production, publishing)
- Thought leadership content (whitepapers, guides, case studies)
- Service page copy optimization
- Tax season content strategy
- E-E-A-T signal development (author bios, credentials, citations)
- Content gap analysis vs. competitors
- Content refresh strategy for existing pages

### 3.5 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) / AI Search

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Optimization for Google AI Overviews (formerly SGE)
- Optimization for ChatGPT / OpenAI search
- Optimization for Perplexity AI
- Structured data strategy for AI citation
- Featured snippet optimization (paragraph, list, table formats)
- E-E-A-T signal optimization for AI extractability
- Content formatting for AI extractability (clear headings, concise answers, scannable structure)
- AI citation tracking and measurement methodology
- Knowledge graph optimization
- Brand entity recognition in AI models
- Voice search optimization (conversational queries, natural language)
- Competitor AI citation analysis

### 3.6 Review & Reputation Management

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Review generation strategy (ethical solicitation methods)
- Review monitoring across platforms
- Response templates and protocols
- Review velocity targets
- Negative review mitigation strategy
- Review integration into website and local SEO
- Multi-platform review strategy (Google, Yelp, industry-specific)

### 3.7 Link Building & Authority

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low / Not Needed]

- Backlink strategy (quality over quantity)
- Digital PR and earned media
- Guest posting on authoritative accounting/tax/finance sites
- Partnership content and co-marketing
- HARO / journalist response strategy
- Industry directory and association links
- Competitor backlink gap analysis

## 6. Compliance & Ethics

_All vendors must confirm adherence to the following standards:_

### 6.1 White-Hat SEO Only

- [ ] No Private Blog Networks (PBNs)
- [ ] No link schemes or paid links (without nofollow)
- [ ] No keyword stuffing or hidden text
- [ ] No cloaking or deceptive redirects
- [ ] No duplicate or scraped content
- [ ] No automated link building tools
- [ ] Full compliance with Google Webmaster Guidelines
- [ ] Full compliance with Bing Webmaster Guidelines

### 6.2 Data Handling & Privacy

- [ ] Client data handled securely (encryption, access controls)
- [ ] No sharing of client data with third parties without consent
- [ ] Compliance with applicable privacy regulations
- [ ] Data retention and deletion policies documented
- [ ] Subcontractor data handling standards defined

### 6.3 Transparency

- [ ] Full disclosure of all tactics and strategies employed
- [ ] No black-box methods — firm can understand what's being done
- [ ] Clear reporting on all work performed
- [ ] Ownership of all accounts, content, and assets transfers to client

## 10. Evaluation Criteria

_Proposals will be evaluated on the following weighted criteria:_

| Criteria | Weight | What We're Evaluating |
|----------|--------|----------------------|
| **CPA/Accounting Domain Expertise** | 25% | Understanding of tax services, CPA buyer journeys, professional services regulations, industry-specific keyword strategy |
| **AEO / AI Search Capability** | 20% | Ability to optimize for AI-powered search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity), structured data for citation, AI citation tracking methodology |
| **Technical SEO Depth** | 15% | Site speed, Core Web Vitals, structured data implementation, crawlability, indexation, schema markup expertise |
| **Content Quality & Strategy** | 15% | Content specificity to CPA firms (not generic), E-E-A-T demonstration, topic cluster methodology, editorial quality |
| **Reporting Transparency** | 10% | Clear KPIs, transparent dashboards, regular reporting cadence, actionable insights, no vanity metrics |
| **Pricing Model** | 10% | Total cost of ownership, value alignment with deliverables, no hidden fees, scalability, contract flexibility |
| **References & Track Record** | 5% | Measurable results from similar CPA firms, quality of references, longevity of client relationships |
