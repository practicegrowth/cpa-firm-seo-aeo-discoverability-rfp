# Local SEO Scorecard — Local Search Optimization Evaluation

> Use this scorecard to evaluate a vendor's local search optimization capabilities. Critical for CPA firms with physical offices, service-area practices, or multi-location firms.

---

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Rating | Definition |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **5** | Exceptional | Comprehensive, proven methodology with specific examples and measurable results |
| **4** | Strong | Solid approach with evidence of success; minor gaps only |
| **3** | Adequate | Basic capability; meets minimum requirements but lacks depth |
| **2** | Weak | Superficial approach; significant gaps; generic tactics |
| **1** | Insufficient | No local SEO capability or awareness |

## Multi-Location Considerations

If your firm has multiple offices, also evaluate:

- [ ] Can the vendor handle multi-location GBP management?
- [ ] Do they have experience with multi-location citation consistency?
- [ ] Can they create location-specific landing pages at scale?
- [ ] Do they provide consolidated reporting across all locations?
- [ ] Can they manage location-specific review generation?
- [ ] Do they understand the complexity of multi-location local SEO?

---

## Red Flags 🚩

- ❌ Suggests submitting to hundreds of low-quality directories
- ❌ Cannot explain the difference between local pack and organic rankings
- ❌ Has no strategy for review generation beyond "ask clients"
- ❌ Suggests creating separate Google Business Profiles for each service (violates guidelines)
- ❌ Cannot name specific local SEO tools they use
- ❌ Promises #1 rankings in the local pack (no one can guarantee this)
- ❌ Suggests keyword-stuffing the Google Business Profile name

---

## Green Flags ✅

- ✅ Comprehensive citation audit before building new citations
- ✅ Emphasis on review quality and response, not just quantity
- ✅ Local landing pages with unique, valuable content (not duplicate)
- ✅ Regular competitor analysis with actionable recommendations
- ✅ Transparent reporting with clear KPIs tied to business outcomes
- ✅ Understanding of state board rules around review solicitation
- ✅ Proactive approach to local spam fighting
