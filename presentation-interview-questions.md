# Presentation Interview Questions — Vendor Selection

> Use these questions during vendor presentations to assess their expertise, methodology, and fit. Ask follow-up questions when answers are vague or generic.

---

## How to Use This Document

**During presentations:**
- Ask 8-12 questions (don't try to cover everything)
- Prioritize questions based on your firm's needs
- Take notes on specificity and depth of answers
- Watch for AI-washing, vague claims, or generic responses

**Red flags during Q&A:**
- Can't explain technical concepts in plain language
- Deflects questions or gives non-answers
- Can't provide specific examples or case studies
- Dismisses AI search as "not important yet"
- Suggests tactics that violate Google guidelines

---

## Strategic & Methodology Questions

### 1. "Walk us through your approach to CPA firm marketing. What makes it different from general small business SEO?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Understanding of professional services regulations (state board rules, advertising restrictions)
- Knowledge of CPA buyer journey (long sales cycle, trust-based, referral-heavy)
- Awareness of seasonal patterns (tax season, year-end planning)
- Industry-specific keyword strategy (not generic "small business SEO")

**Red flags:**
- Generic "we do SEO for small businesses" response
- No mention of professional services constraints
- Can't explain CPA-specific challenges

---

### 2. "How do you measure success? What KPIs do you track and report on?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Business outcomes (leads, conversions, revenue) not just vanity metrics (rankings, traffic)
- Clear reporting cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- Transparent dashboards (you have access, not just PDF reports)
- AI search citation tracking (if they claim AEO capability)

**Red flags:**
- Focuses only on rankings or traffic
- Can't explain how rankings translate to leads
- No mention of conversion tracking
- Vague about reporting methodology

---

### 3. "What's your philosophy on content quality vs. quantity? How do you ensure content demonstrates E-E-A-T?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Quality over quantity (10 great pieces > 50 generic posts)
- E-E-A-T strategy (author bios, credentials, citations, about pages)
- Topic cluster methodology (pillar pages + supporting content)
- Understanding that CPA content must be accurate and authoritative

**Red flags:**
- Suggests high volume of low-quality content
- Can't explain E-E-A-T or why it matters
- No strategy for author attribution or credential display
- Suggests AI-generated content without human review

---

## Technical SEO Questions

### 4. "What technical SEO issues do you typically find on CPA firm websites? Walk us through your audit process."

**What you're looking for:**
- Specific technical issues (site speed, mobile, structured data, crawlability)
- Core Web Vitals optimization (LCP, INP, CLS)
- Structured data implementation (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ, HowTo, Article)
- Tools they use (Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, Lighthouse)

**Red flags:**
- Can't name specific technical issues
- Doesn't mention structured data or schema markup
- No mention of Core Web Vitals
- Vague about audit methodology

---

### 5. "How do you approach structured data and schema markup? What types do you implement for CPA firms?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Specific schema types: Organization, LocalBusiness, ProfessionalService, Person (for CPAs), Article, FAQ, HowTo, BreadcrumbList
- Understanding of how structured data affects AI search citations
- Examples of schema they've implemented for other CPA firms
- Knowledge of JSON-LD format

**Red flags:**
- Can't name specific schema types
- Doesn't understand relationship between structured data and AI search
- Suggests only basic schema (Organization, LocalBusiness)
- Can't explain technical implementation

---

## AEO / AI Search Questions

### 6. "How do you optimize for AI-powered search like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity? What's your AEO methodology?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Understanding of how AI models select and cite sources
- Content formatting for AI extractability (clear headings, concise answers, scannable structure)
- Structured data strategy for AI citation
- Tools for AI citation tracking (Profound, Otterly, custom methodologies)
- E-E-A-T optimization for AI models

**Red flags:**
- "AEO is just like SEO" (it's not)
- Can't explain how AI search differs from traditional search
- No methodology for tracking AI citations
- Dismisses AI search as "not important yet" (it's 2026)
- Suggests "gaming" AI models rather than building authority

**Follow-up:** "Can you show us an example of a client you've helped appear in AI Overviews or AI chat responses?"

---

### 7. "How do you measure AI search visibility? What tools do you use to track citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Specific tools or methodologies for AI citation tracking
- Baseline measurement of current AI visibility
- Reporting cadence for AI metrics
- Ability to show trend data over time

**Red flags:**
- No tools or methodology for AI tracking
- Can't explain how they'd measure AI visibility
- Suggests it's "too early" to track AI citations
- Vague about reporting

---

## Local SEO Questions

### 8. "What's your approach to local SEO for CPA firms with multiple offices? How do you optimize Google Business Profiles?"

**What you're looking for:**
- GBP optimization depth (categories, services, posts, Q&A, photos)
- NAP consistency across directories
- Citation building strategy (industry-specific + general directories)
- Local landing page strategy (service-area pages, location-specific content)
- Review generation approach (ethical solicitation, response templates)

**Red flags:**
- Suggests creating separate GBPs for each service (violates guidelines)
- Can't explain local pack vs. organic rankings
- No strategy for review generation beyond "ask clients"
- Suggests submitting to hundreds of low-quality directories

---

### 9. "How do you handle review generation for CPA firms? What's compliant with state board rules?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Understanding of state board restrictions on review solicitation
- Ethical solicitation methods (email, SMS, in-person)
- Response templates for positive and negative reviews
- Review velocity targets (realistic goals)
- Multi-platform strategy (Google, Yelp, industry-specific)

**Red flags:**
- Suggests incentivizing reviews (violates FTC guidelines)
- Can't explain state board restrictions
- No strategy for handling negative reviews
- Suggests fake reviews or review gating

---

## Content & Thought Leadership Questions

### 10. "How do you develop content that positions our firm as a thought leader? What's your editorial process?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Topic cluster methodology (pillar pages + supporting content)
- Tax season content strategy (seasonal planning)
- Thought leadership content (whitepapers, guides, case studies)
- Editorial calendar approach (frequency, topic selection)
- Content quality standards (writing depth, accuracy, professionalism)

**Red flags:**
- Suggests generic "10 Tax Tips" content
- No editorial calendar or content strategy
- Can't explain topic clusters
- Suggests AI-generated content without human review

---

### 11. "How do you ensure content is accurate and compliant with professional standards? What's your review process?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Fact-checking process (citing IRS publications, state regulations)
- Review workflow (content writer → CPA review → publish)
- Understanding that CPA content must be accurate (not just SEO-optimized)
- Update cadence for existing content (tax law changes)

**Red flags:**
- No review process or fact-checking
- Suggests publishing without CPA review
- Can't explain how they'd ensure accuracy
- No strategy for updating content when laws change

---

## Pricing & Contract Questions

### 12. "Walk us through your pricing model. What's included in your retainer? What are additional costs?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Transparent pricing (no hidden fees)
- Clear breakdown of what's included (retainer, content production, tools, reporting)
- Contract flexibility (month-to-month vs. long-term lock-in)
- Scalability (can grow with firm without renegotiation)

**Red flags:**
- Vague about pricing or "it depends" without specifics
- Hidden fees for tools, content, or reporting
- Long-term contracts with auto-renewal
- Can't explain what's included vs. additional

---

### 13. "What's your minimum contract length? What happens if we're not satisfied?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Month-to-month or short-term contracts (6 months max)
- Clear termination clauses (30-day notice)
- No auto-renewal traps
- Performance guarantees or SLAs

**Red flags:**
- 12-month minimum with auto-renewal
- No termination clause or difficult exit
- Can't explain what happens if you're not satisfied
- Suggests you "won't want to leave" (confidence, but also lock-in)

---

## References & Track Record Questions

### 14. "Can you provide 2-3 references from CPA firms you've worked with? What results did you achieve?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Specific case studies with measurable results (traffic, leads, revenue)
- References from similar-sized firms
- Longevity of client relationships (retention rate)
- Willingness to provide references (not just "trust us")

**Red flags:**
- Can't provide references
- References are not from CPA firms
- Vague about results ("we improved their SEO")
- Client relationships are short-term

---

### 15. "What's your client retention rate? Why do clients stay with you? Why do clients leave?"

**What you're looking for:**
- High retention rate (80%+ is good)
- Honest about why clients leave (budget constraints, firm mergers, etc.)
- Long-term client relationships (3+ years)
- Transparent about challenges

**Red flags:**
- Can't provide retention rate
- Claims 100% retention (unlikely)
- Blames clients for leaving ("they didn't understand SEO")
- Short-term client relationships (average < 2 years)

---

## Cultural Fit Questions

### 16. "How do you communicate with clients? What's your reporting cadence?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Regular communication (weekly calls, monthly reports)
- Transparent reporting (dashboards, not just PDFs)
- Responsive to questions (24-48 hour response time)
- Proactive recommendations (not just order-takers)

**Red flags:**
- Vague about communication cadence
- Suggests "we'll send reports" without regular calls
- Can't explain how they'd handle urgent questions
- Suggests you "won't need to call us often" (not good)

---

### 17. "Who will be our day-to-day contact? Who's on our team?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Dedicated account manager (not rotating staff)
- Clear team structure (account lead, SEO strategist, content lead, technical SEO)
- Experience level of team members
- Backup plan if account manager leaves

**Red flags:**
- Can't name specific team members
- Suggests "you'll work with our team" without specifics
- High turnover or rotating staff
- Junior staff handling your account

---

## Closing Questions

### 18. "Why should we choose you over other vendors? What makes you different?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Specific differentiators (not just "we're the best")
- CPA/accounting specialization
- AEO/AI search capability
- Measurable results from similar firms
- Cultural fit with your firm

**Red flags:**
- Generic "we're the best" response
- Can't articulate specific differentiators
- No CPA specialization
- Can't explain why they're different

---

### 19. "If we hire you, what does the first 90 days look like? What should we expect?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Clear onboarding process (audit, strategy, implementation)
- Realistic timeline (SEO takes 6-12 months)
- Quick wins (technical fixes, GBP optimization)
- Long-term strategy (content, link building, AEO)

**Red flags:**
- Promises instant results
- No clear onboarding process
- Can't explain what happens in first 90 days
- Suggests you'll see results in 30 days (unlikely)

---

### 20. "What questions do you have for us?"

**What you're looking for:**
- Thoughtful questions about your firm, goals, challenges
- Demonstrates they've done research
- Shows genuine interest in your success
- Asks about decision-making process, timeline, budget

**Red flags:**
- No questions (lack of interest)
- Generic questions you've already answered
- Only asks about budget (not about goals or challenges)
- Can't articulate next steps
