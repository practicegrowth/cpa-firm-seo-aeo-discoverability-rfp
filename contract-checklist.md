# Contract Checklist — SEO & Discoverability Vendor Agreement

> Use this checklist when reviewing and negotiating your vendor contract. Ensure all critical terms are covered before signing.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

**Before signing:**
- Review each item below
- Ensure it's addressed in the contract
- If missing, request it be added
- Have legal counsel review (especially for IP, termination, liability clauses)

**Red flags in contracts:**
- Auto-renewal without notice
- Difficult termination clauses
- IP ownership unclear
- No performance guarantees
- Vague scope of work

---

## 1. Scope of Work

### 1.1 Detailed Deliverables

- [ ] **Specific services listed** — Technical SEO, on-page, local, content, AEO, reviews, link building
- [ ] **Deliverables defined** — What exactly will be delivered (e.g., "4 blog posts per month," "monthly technical audit")
- [ ] **Exclusions listed** — What's NOT included (prevents scope creep)
- [ ] **Content production** — Number of pieces, word counts, review process, approval workflow
- [ ] **Technical work** — Specific fixes, schema implementation, site speed optimization
- [ ] **Reporting** — Frequency, format, metrics included, dashboard access

### 1.2 Timeline & Milestones

- [ ] **Project phases** — Audit, implementation, content, measurement
- [ ] **Milestone dates** — When each phase starts and ends
- [ ] **Quick wins timeline** — When to expect initial improvements (30-60 days)
- [ ] **Long-term goals** — 6-month and 12-month targets
- [ ] **Content calendar** — When content will be delivered and published

### 1.3 Communication & Reporting

- [ ] **Meeting cadence** — Weekly calls, monthly strategy sessions
- [ ] **Reporting frequency** — Weekly, monthly, quarterly reports
- [ ] **Report format** — Dashboard access, PDF reports, both
- [ ] **Response time** — How quickly they respond to questions (24-48 hours)
- [ ] **Point of contact** — Dedicated account manager name and contact info
- [ ] **Escalation process** — What happens if there's an issue or dispute

---

## 2. Pricing & Payment Terms

### 2.1 Fee Structure

- [ ] **Monthly retainer** — Exact amount, what's included
- [ ] **One-time fees** — Audit, setup, onboarding costs
- [ ] **Content costs** — Per-piece pricing if not included in retainer
- [ ] **Tool costs** — Are tools included or billed separately?
- [ ] **Additional costs** — Any other fees (travel, subcontractors, etc.)

### 2.2 Payment Terms

- [ ] **Payment schedule** — When payments are due (monthly, quarterly)
- [ ] **Payment method** — Credit card, ACH, check
- [ ] **Late payment terms** — Penalties for late payment (should be reasonable)
- [ ] **Price increases** — How much notice for price changes (90 days minimum)
- [ ] **Price lock** — How long is pricing guaranteed?

### 2.3 Budget Controls

- [ ] **Overage approval** — Process if work exceeds budget (must get approval first)
- [ ] **Content limits** — What happens if you need more content than planned?
- [ ] **Hourly rates** — If work is billed hourly, what are the rates?
- [ ] **Cap on hours** — Maximum hours per month without additional approval

---

## 3. Contract Terms

### 3.1 Contract Length

- [ ] **Initial term** — 6 months preferred (12 months max)
- [ ] **Month-to-month option** — After initial term, can you go month-to-month?
- [ ] **Auto-renewal** — Does it auto-renew? How much notice to cancel?
- [ ] **Renewal terms** — Are renewal prices the same or can they increase?

### 3.2 Termination Clauses

- [ ] **Termination for convenience** — Can you terminate without cause? (30-60 days notice)
- [ ] **Termination for cause** — What constitutes cause (non-performance, breach, etc.)
- [ ] **Notice period** — How much notice required (30 days standard)
- [ ] **Early termination fee** — Are there penalties for early termination? (Should be minimal or none)
- [ ] **Transition support** — Will they help transition to new vendor? (Should be included)

### 3.3 Performance Guarantees

- [ ] **SLAs** — Service level agreements (response times, deliverable deadlines)
- [ ] **Performance metrics** — What happens if they don't meet agreed-upon metrics?
- [ ] **Remedies** — What recourse do you have if they underperform?
- [ ] **Exit clause** — Can you terminate if they consistently underperform?

---

## 4. Intellectual Property & Ownership

### 4.1 Content Ownership

- [ ] **You own everything** — All content created for you is yours (blog posts, pages, etc.)
- [ ] **Work-for-hire** — Contract states work is "work made for hire" under copyright law
- [ ] **Full rights transfer** — They assign all rights to you upon creation
- [ ] **Source files** — You receive source files (Word docs, design files, etc.)
- [ ] **No reuse** — They can't reuse your content for other clients

### 4.2 Technical Work

- [ ] **Code ownership** — Any custom code, scripts, or technical work is yours
- [ ] **Schema markup** — All structured data implementation is yours
- [ ] **Website changes** — All website modifications are yours
- [ ] **Documentation** — They provide documentation of all work performed

### 4.3 Accounts & Access

- [ ] **Account ownership** — You own all accounts (Google Business Profile, analytics, etc.)
- [ ] **Access transfer** — They'll transfer all access and credentials at end of engagement
- [ ] **No hold hostage** — They can't hold accounts or content hostage if you terminate
- [ ] **Data export** — They'll provide all data, reports, and analytics in exportable format

---

## 5. Confidentiality & Data Handling

### 5.1 Confidentiality

- [ ] **NDA clause** — They'll keep your business information confidential
- [ ] **Client data** — They won't share your client data with third parties
- [ ] **Business strategies** — They won't disclose your marketing strategies to competitors
- [ ] **Duration** — Confidentiality obligations survive termination (2-3 years typical)

### 5.2 Data Security

- [ ] **Data handling** — How they store and protect your data
- [ ] **Access controls** — Who has access to your accounts and data?
- [ ] **Breach notification** — They'll notify you if there's a data breach (within 48 hours)
- [ ] **Data deletion** — They'll delete your data after engagement ends (upon request)

### 5.3 Subcontractors

- [ ] **Subcontractor disclosure** — Will they use subcontractors? Who are they?
- [ ] **Subcontractor standards** — Subcontractors must meet same confidentiality standards
- [ ] **Approval required** — You approve subcontractors before they work on your account
- [ ] **Liability** — Vendor is liable for subcontractor work

---

## 6. Compliance & Ethics

### 6.1 White-Hat SEO

- [ ] **Google guidelines** — They'll follow Google Webmaster Guidelines
- [ ] **No black-hat tactics** — No PBNs, link schemes, keyword stuffing, cloaking
- [ ] **No guaranteed rankings** — They won't guarantee #1 rankings (no one can)
- [ ] **Transparency** — They'll disclose all tactics and strategies used

### 6.2 Professional Standards

- [ ] **State board compliance** — They understand CPA advertising restrictions
- [ ] **FTC compliance** — Review practices comply with FTC guidelines
- [ ] **Privacy compliance** — They comply with applicable privacy laws (CCPA, GDPR if applicable)
- [ ] **Industry standards** — They follow professional services marketing best practices

### 6.3 Ethical Standards

- [ ] **No conflicts of interest** — They won't work with direct competitors (or disclose if they do)
- [ ] **Honest reporting** — Reports are accurate and not manipulated
- [ ] **No fake reviews** — They won't create fake reviews or use review gating
- [ ] **Transparency** — Full disclosure of all work performed

---

## 7. Liability & Indemnification

### 7.1 Liability Limits

- [ ] **Liability cap** — Their liability is limited to fees paid (reasonable)
- [ ] **Your liability** — Your liability is limited to fees owed (reasonable)
- [ ] **Consequential damages** — Neither party liable for consequential damages (standard)
- [ ] **Insurance** — They carry professional liability insurance (ask for certificate)

### 7.2 Indemnification

- [ ] **Their indemnification** — They'll indemnify you if their work infringes on third-party rights
- [ ] **Your indemnification** — You'll indemnify them if you provide infringing content
- [ ] **Mutual indemnification** — Both parties protected (balanced)

### 7.3 Dispute Resolution

- [ ] **Governing law** — Which state's law governs the contract?
- [ ] **Dispute process** — Mediation → Arbitration → Litigation (escalating)
- [ ] **Venue** — Where disputes are resolved (your state preferred)
- [ ] **Attorney fees** — Prevailing party gets attorney fees (deters frivolous claims)

---

## 8. Additional Provisions

### 8.1 Force Majeure

- [ ] **Force majeure clause** — Neither party liable for events beyond control (pandemics, natural disasters)
- [ ] **Notification** — Must notify other party within X days of force majeure event
- [ ] **Termination right** — Can terminate if force majeure lasts more than X days (90 days typical)

### 8.2 Assignment & Transfer

- [ ] **No assignment without consent** — They can't transfer your contract to another company without your approval
- [ ] **Change of control** — If they're acquired, you can terminate or approve new owner

### 8.3 Entire Agreement

- [ ] **Entire agreement clause** — Contract represents entire agreement (no side deals)
- [ ] **Amendments** — All changes must be in writing and signed by both parties
- [ ] **Severability** — If one clause is invalid, rest of contract still applies

---

## 9. Red Flags in Contracts 🚩

**Avoid contracts with:**

- ❌ **Auto-renewal without notice** — Should require affirmative renewal
- ❌ **Difficult termination** — Can't be locked into a bad relationship
- ❌ **IP ownership unclear** — Must be crystal clear that you own everything
- ❌ **No performance guarantees** — What recourse do you have if they underperform?
- ❌ **Vague scope** — "SEO services" is not specific enough
- ❌ **Excessive liability caps** — Should be reasonable for both parties
- ❌ **Non-compete clauses** — They shouldn't restrict who you can hire in the future
- ❌ **Hidden fees** — All costs should be transparent
- ❌ **Long-term lock-in** — 12+ months with no exit is risky
- ❌ **No transition support** — They should help you transition if you terminate

## 10. Negotiation Tips

### 10.1 What to Negotiate

- **Price** — Ask for discounts for longer commitments or annual payment
- **Term length** — Push for shorter initial term (6 months, not 12)
- **Termination** — Negotiate easier termination (30 days, no penalty)
- **Scope** — Get more included in retainer (content, tools, reporting)
- **SLAs** — Add performance guarantees with remedies
- **IP ownership** — Ensure you own everything (non-negotiable)

### 10.2 What's Non-Negotiable

- **IP ownership** — You must own all content and work product
- **White-hat SEO** — No black-hat tactics (period)
- **Transparency** — Full disclosure of all work performed
- **Account ownership** — You own all accounts and access
- **Confidentiality** — Your data and strategies are protected

### 10.3 Getting It in Writing

- **All promises in contract** — If it's not in the contract, it doesn't exist
- **Specific deliverables** — "4 blog posts per month" not "regular content"
- **Clear timelines** — Specific dates, not "as soon as possible"
- **Defined metrics** — Measurable KPIs, not vague goals

---

## 11. Final Review Checklist

**Before signing, confirm:**

- [ ] Scope of work is specific and detailed
- [ ] Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees
- [ ] Contract term is reasonable (6-12 months)
- [ ] Termination is easy (30-60 days notice)
- [ ] You own all content and work product
- [ ] Accounts and access transfer to you
- [ ] Confidentiality and data handling are covered
- [ ] Compliance and ethics standards are clear
- [ ] Liability and indemnification are balanced
- [ ] Dispute resolution process is defined
- [ ] All verbal promises are in the contract
- [ ] Legal counsel has reviewed (recommended)

---

## 12. Post-Signature Actions

**After signing:**

- [ ] **Schedule kickoff meeting** — Align on goals, timeline, communication
- [ ] **Provide access** — CMS, analytics, Google Business Profile, etc.
- [ ] **Share brand guidelines** — Messaging, tone, visual identity
- [ ] **Establish baseline metrics** — Current state documented
- [ ] **Set communication cadence** — Weekly calls, monthly reports
- [ ] **Define success metrics** — What does success look like in 6/12 months?
- [ ] **Create shared workspace** — Google Drive, Slack, project management tool
- [ ] **Introduce teams** — Your team meets their team
